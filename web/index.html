Lightweight CPU VTuber tracker: browser preview

Try face, head and arm tracking on a VRM avatar with your webcam. Nothing is uploaded: the video stays in this tab.

This preview is not the real tracker. It runs MediaPipe Tasks in JavaScript to show the idea. The real one is native Rust with an InstantHMR body model, tracked fingers, and 26 ms median latency on a laptop CPU. Download it from Releases.
