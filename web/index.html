Lightweight CPU VTuber tracker: browser preview

Face, head, arm and finger tracking on a VRM avatar from your webcam. The video never leaves this tab: nothing is uploaded.

This page runs the app's own models and tracking code, ported to the browser: BlazeFace, Face Mesh and the blendshape model for the face, RF-DETR nano and InstantHMR v1.1 nano with the MHR body model for the body, the MediaPipe 2023 palm and hand models for fingers, the same One Euro smoothing, the same arm, spine and finger solver. On the same frames the output matches the native Rust app (parity numbers are in web/body, web/face and web/hands READMEs on GitHub). What differs: one-thread WebAssembly on the CPU (no GPU), so it is slower than the native app, and the numbers below are measured in your browser; the avatar is drawn with three.js instead of Bevy.
